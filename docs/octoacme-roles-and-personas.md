# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons manage communication between project teams and external stakeholders. They gather and communicate stakeholder expectations, escalate concerns, and ensure alignment on project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Manage communication between project teams and external stakeholders
- Gather and communicate stakeholder expectations and feedback
- Escalate concerns and issues to appropriate project leadership
- Provide regular status updates to stakeholders
- Facilitate stakeholder reviews and approval gates

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder satisfaction and alignment
- Reduce miscommunication and unmet expectations
- Enable efficient decision-making through clear communication channels

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Project Manager** on communication strategy and escalation protocols
- **Coordinates with Quality Assurance Lead** on acceptance criteria and stakeholder testing needs
- **Collaborates with Release Manager** on deployment notifications and release communications

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and status updates
- Requirement clarification sessions
- Escalation and decision documentation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and oversee testing strategies and quality processes. They ensure deliverables meet quality standards and acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and quality standards for projects
- Oversee QA processes, including manual and automated testing
- Manage test documentation, test cases, and automation frameworks
- Approve quality gates and ensure defects are tracked and resolved
- Identify, track, and report on quality metrics and test coverage

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality deliverables that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce defects in production through comprehensive testing
- Maintain and improve test automation and quality processes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with Product Managers** on requirements clarity and acceptance criteria definition
- **Supports Development Team** on testing protocols, test-driven development practices, and code quality standards
- **Coordinates with Project Manager** on testing timelines and resource planning
- **Coordinates with Release Manager** on UAT timelines and release readiness validation

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and QA strategy documentation
- Test case reviews and defect reports
- Quality metrics and test coverage reports
- Pre-release quality assessments

---

## Infrastructure/DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Infrastructure and DevOps Engineers manage deployment infrastructure, ensure system reliability and performance, and support incident response. They enable teams to deploy reliably and maintain production systems.

### Responsibilities
- Manage deployment infrastructure and environments (dev, staging, production)
- Ensure system reliability, performance, and scalability
- Handle infrastructure scaling and auto-recovery mechanisms
- Set up monitoring, logging, and alerting for production systems
- Support incident response and troubleshooting

### Goals
- Enable reliable and frequent deployments
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce deployment risks and incident response time

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Collaborates with Development Team** on deployment requirements, dependency management, and environment configuration
- **Works with Project Manager** on release scheduling and deployment planning
- **Works with Release Manager** on release coordination and deployment execution
- **Partners with Security Officer** on infrastructure hardening, security patches, and compliance requirements

### Typical Communication
- Infrastructure architecture and design documentation
- Deployment runbooks and procedures
- Monitoring and incident reports
- Capacity planning and scaling recommendations

---

## Security Officer

### Role Summary
Security Officers ensure that projects meet security and compliance requirements. They conduct security assessments, manage vulnerabilities, and approve deployments from a security perspective.

### Responsibilities
- Review and define security requirements for projects
- Conduct security assessments and code reviews
- Manage compliance requirements and audit processes
- Identify, track, and remediate security vulnerabilities
- Approve deployments and releases from a security perspective

### Goals
- Protect systems and data from security threats
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and industry standards
- Build security into every stage of the development lifecycle

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with Product Managers** on security requirements and features
- **Reviews code and design with Development Team** on security best practices and vulnerability prevention
- **Coordinates with Infrastructure/DevOps Engineer** on infrastructure security hardening and security patching
- **Works with Project Manager** on security timelines and risk management
- **Works with Release Manager** on security validation and approval before production deployment

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement specifications
- Vulnerability reports and remediation tracking
- Security assessments and audit documentation
- Security approval gates for releases
